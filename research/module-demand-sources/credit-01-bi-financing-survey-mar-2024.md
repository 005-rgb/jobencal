**N****o. 26/84/DKom**

The latest Bank Financing Supply and Demand Survey conducted by Bank Indonesia in March 2024 indicates increasing corporate demand for financing, as reflected by a higher Weighted Net Balance (WNB) of 25.3% in the reporting period compared with 11.1% in February 2024. This was primarily driven by stronger demand in the trade sector, manufacturing industry, and construction sector. Corporate demand for financing was predominantly sourced from internal funds, followed by committed undisbursed loans and financing from domestic banks.

In terms of households, 10.9% of respondents acknowledged demand for new financing in March 2024. Most household financing was sourced from commercial banks, with a 41.2% share in the reporting period, up from 39.8% the month earlier. In addition to the banking industry, households also relied on other sources to meet their financing needs, including cooperatives and leasing.

Meanwhile, new loan disbursements by the banking industry in March 2024 also tracked an upward trend, with the corresponding WNB increasing to 80.9% from 54.1% in February 2024. The main factors affecting new loan disbursements in the reporting period were customer demand for financing, the monetary and economic outlook as well as the level of competition between banking institutions. Overall, respondents expect to maintain positive growth of new loan disbursements in the first quarter of 2024.

More information concerning the [Bank Financing Supply and Demand Survey](https://www.bi.go.id/id/covid19/Pages/Survei-Penawaran-dan-Permintaan-Pembayaran-Perbankan-Maret-2024.aspx) is available on the Bank Indonesia website.
